# RSNA 15 → 16: controlled 0.943+B2 probe

## Notebook 15 decision

`results_15.zip` is `COMPLETE` on all five original folds and matches notebook
09 on 4,349 weak UIDs, fold assignments, encoder SHA256, and 327,497 images.
Yet notebook 15 weak OOF macro AUC is **0.816646** versus notebook 09
**0.858970** (delta **−0.042324**). Validation BCE is worse on all five
folds; all 12 weak-label target AUCs decline. Gold58 audit-only macro AUC is
0.842161 versus 0.888217. Do not submit or blend notebook 15. This does not
disprove native consecutive-slice 2.5D with an adapted encoder; notebook 15
used sampled cache neighbors with a frozen encoder.

## New CPU-only diagnostic

`analyze_16_public_oof_b2_diagnostic.py` reads existing ZIPs without changing
them. It aligns notebook 09 B2 with public DINO and E9/E11/E13 OOF candidates
by exact StudyInstanceUID on the 4,349 L1 weak studies. A deliberately fixed
partial DINO+E13 rank proxy has weak-label macro AUC **0.863469**; adding 20%
B2 rank gives **0.870346** (+0.006877). The delta is positive on all five B2
folds and all 12 targets. This is evidence of complementarity to the *partial*
public proxy, **not** OOF of the 0.943 ensemble: A5, Raptor, and all CoAt
families are missing. L1 labels are report-derived; public checkpoint OOF
provenance is not independently verified. No Private-LB estimate follows.

## Notebook 16

`16_RSNA_0943_Anchor_Plus_B2_Fixed20_Probe.ipynb` is one controlled Public-LB
probe. It copies the exact code cells of the protected 0.943 notebook and the
successful notebook 10 B2 standalone inference. A lightweight B2 attachment
precheck runs first. After anchor inference it preserves the exact anchor
`submission.csv` and checks its SHA256, 20 DINO members, five A5 folds, four
Raptor views and four CoAt members. B2 then verifies the five notebook 09 head
checkpoints and RadImageNet encoder hash. The final cell validates exact UID,
column and row alignment, then writes:

`rank(0.80 × rank(anchor_0943) + 0.20 × rank(B2_09))`

No model is trained and no weight is searched in this notebook. The original
0.943 notebook and selected submission remain untouched. A visible three-study
output can have the same ranking as the anchor even if B2 runs; the hidden
rerun may behave differently. The combined hidden runtime is unknown and may
exceed either standalone notebook's runtime.
